/help

Phonebook is entirely managed from your messaging applications installed on your phone, such as SMS or WhatsApp. More apps, including but not limited to Telegram, are coming soon.

To get help from Phonebook, simply send the command /help, and you will receive a list of available commands for various actions and activities.

Currently, the following commands are available:

/info: Get information about your Phonebook website, such as your unique URL and current settings.

/settings: Set up a custom domain, change your profile photo, and adjust other preferences for your Phonebook site.

/analytics: Retrieve statistics for your Phonebook site, including visitor count, page views, and engagement metrics.

/delete: Remove selected items from your Phonebook site to keep your content up-to-date.

More commands and features will be added in the future to enhance your Phonebook experience.
